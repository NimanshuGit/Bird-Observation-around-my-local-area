# Bird-Observation-around-my-local-area

This repository is part of my Dear 100 data collection project, where I record bird activity around my local area during the afternoon hours (12:00 PM – 5:00 PM).

Type of data Points which are being Recorded

-->Timestamp: The exact time of day when the birds are observed.
-->Bird Type / Species:** The category or species of birds observed (e.g., Pigeon, Crow, Sparrow).
-->Group Size: The total number of birds in the group.
--> Primary Action: What the birds are doing during the observation (e.g., Foraging, Perched, currently flying, Calling).
--> Height / Level: Where the birds are observed spatially (Ground level, Eye level, or on Roofs/Structures).
--> Environment: The weather and atmospheric conditions at the time of observation (e.g., Sunny, noisy,rainy).


The reason of data collection

Data collection on backyard birds combines personal passion with real-world data collection for conservation. With inspired by Bear Grylls' hands-on relationship with nature, logging local birds brings the thrill of tracking wildlife right to your doorstep, particularly if you're not dealing with bigger animals. Keeping a yard list is a fun activity for committed birders that can help you learn how to attract wildlife, record the rare birds that call your yard home, and spot the subtle seasonal changes firsthand.

These are daily observations that directly affect citizen science as well as for personal enjoyment. Controlling migrations, assessing population health and determining the effects of urbanization on wildlife with statistical data reported to global databases such as eBird. Your own hobby can be valuable to larger ecological studies by recording birds near your home.
